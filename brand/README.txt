TAHA BUILDERS — LOGO FILES
Mark: "Channel", a dredged channel section cut through the waterline.

COLOURS
  Navy   #12305C   the block
  Teal   #5FB2AE   the waterline
  White  #FFFFFF   the cut
  Deep navy #0A1B33  dark backgrounds

WHICH FILE TO USE
  ../images/logo-mark.svg           on white and light backgrounds. Use this by default.
  ../images/logo-mark-reversed.svg  on navy and dark photographs.
  ../images/logo-mark-mono.svg      one colour, for stamps, faxes, engraving, embroidery.
  logo-mark-square.svg              sharp-corner variant, if the client prefers no rounding.

  PNGs are provided at 1024, 512, 256, 180 and 96 px for anyone who cannot use SVG.
  Always prefer the SVG. It stays sharp at any size, including on a vessel hull.

FAVICON
  ../favicon.ico plus ../images/favicon-16/32/48/180.png, already linked in every page.

WORDMARK
  Typeface is Inter Tight Bold for "TAHA BUILDERS", Inter Semibold with wide letter
  spacing for "DREDGING & CONSTRUCTION". Both are free for commercial use and are
  already self-hosted in the site's fonts/ folder.

CLEAR SPACE
  Keep empty space around the mark equal to the height of the teal band, on all sides.

DO NOT
  Stretch it, rotate it, add a shadow or gradient, recolour it outside the palette,
  or place the light version on a dark photograph. Use the reversed file instead.

BEFORE THIS GOES ON ANYTHING PERMANENT
  Have a designer refine the curves and spacing, and have the name and mark cleared
  for trademark in Bangladesh.
