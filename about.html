About Taha Builders

An infrastructure and civil engineering contractor working across Bangladesh, from riverbed to structural handover.

Taha Builders is an infrastructure and civil engineering contractor based in Chattogram. Two decades of riverine, civil and transport work, delivered with owned plant, in-house engineering and a record measured in thousands of crore of executed contracts.

The work spans dredging, land reclamation, highways and heavy civil construction, for port authorities, government bodies and international prime contractors.

Track Record

Value of work executed, by discipline.

DredgingTk 200+ crore

Riverbed excavation, maintenance dredging and channel navigation restoration across major waterways, including high-capacity contracts for port authorities and government bodies.

Sand filling & land reclamationTk 1,200–1,300 crore

Large-scale land development, sand supply and site preparation for economic zones, mega power plants and industrial complexes.

Highways & roadsTk 300+ crore

Flexible and rigid pavements, national highway binding courses and urban road networks.

Civil & protective worksTk 200+ crore

Heavy structural civil works, multi-storey developments, riverbank slope protection, retaining walls and shoreline erosion control.

Why Work With Us

What a client gets that a general contractor cannot offer.

Integrated capability

Deep river dredging, land development and structural handover managed under one roof, across the full project lifecycle, so a single contract covers the whole scope.

Owned fleet, in-house engineering

Cutter suction dredgers and heavy earthmoving plant operated by certified engineering teams, under set quality and safety protocols. Mobilisation dates are ours to control.

Trusted by public clients

A record of collaboration with government entities, autonomous port authorities and international EPC prime contractors.
