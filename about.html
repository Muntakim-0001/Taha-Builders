WHO WE ARE

Building Bangladesh's Infrastructure

Building infrastructure, marine solutions, and strategic partnerships since 2007.

TAHA Builders, established in 2007, is a Bangladesh-based company focused on infrastructure, construction, dredging and marine-related project execution.

We work with clients, contractors, suppliers and strategic partners to support the delivery of complex infrastructure and development projects. Our approach combines local market knowledge, project coordination, technical capabilities and strategic business relationships.

Our objective is to build long-term relationships with government agencies, private-sector organizations, international companies and technical partners while maintaining a strong commitment to quality, safety, reliability and responsible project execution.

Local Expertise

Responsible Execution

Our Vision

Excellence, strategic partnerships, and sustainable infrastructure development.

“To build a trusted infrastructure company recognized for excellence in project execution, strategic partnerships and sustainable development.”

We aim to contribute to the development of Bangladesh's infrastructure by building long-term capabilities, establishing strong international relationships and participating in projects that create lasting economic and social value.
