HSE & Quality

Health, Safety, Environment & Quality Management Framework at Taha Builders.

At TAHA Builders, maintaining high standards of health, safety, environmental stewardship, and quality management is central to responsible project execution across all marine and infrastructure operations.

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)

We are committed to maintaining responsible practices across our project activities, with a focus on protecting our people, project stakeholders, marine life, and local environments in Bangladesh.

Quality Management

Quality is integrated into every phase of our approach — from initial survey, engineering planning, equipment deployment, and project coordination to final handover.

Our Core Priorities

Four pillars guiding disciplined risk management and operational standards.

01

Health & Safety

Protecting employees, site operators, contractors, and project stakeholders through continuous training and strict site protocols.

02

Quality Assurance

Maintaining consistent material, structural, and dredging execution standards throughout project delivery.

03

Environmental Responsibility

Managing marine and civil project activities responsibly to minimize ecological footprint and protect river habitats.

04

Risk Management

Identifying and mitigating operational, geological, and environmental risks through structured controls.
